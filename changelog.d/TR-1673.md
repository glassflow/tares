The Report has an Architecture section (TR-1673). It shows, for each repo, how many architecture findings
review first raised each week, marks a repo as Rising after three rises in a row over its last four
finished weeks, and lists the decisions made in those weeks beside their repo. Architecture is a new kind of
review finding, matched by the words of the finding, such as layering or a module boundary. Findings
recorded before this kind existed are not counted.
