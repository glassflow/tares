Changed
Projects are plain records with a name, goal, icon, status, lead and members (TR-1812). There are no project templates any more: `POST /api/projects` accepts a `template`, `params` or `objects` field and ignores it, every new project is a custom one, and `PUT /api/projects/{id}` changes the name, goal and icon only. The template list (/api/projects/templates), the pre-1.14 /api/usecases routes and the seeded first-boot project are gone, and TARES_SEED_PROJECT and TARES_SEED_USECASE are no longer read. A project's card no longer carries `template_title`, `recipe`, `recipe_title` or `setup`, and deleting a project answers `{"ok": true}`.
