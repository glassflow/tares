Added
The Second Brain has a Decisions page (Knowledge, Decisions): every decision the brain knows, with its origin (a factory project, a GitHub repo or the Second Brain itself), who decided and when, in tabs with the cell's counts. Record a decision (top right) opens a form at `/brain/decisions/new` where anyone whose key can write adds one, pasted or as a Markdown, text or PDF file, and the person who added one or an admin deletes it. `?repo=owner/name` narrows the list to one repo. On a project, "Decisions and calls" is now "Crew questions", with the tabs "Answered by the crew" and "Answered by you".
