Added
The Second Brain has a Decisions page (Knowledge, Decisions): every decision the brain knows, from repo folders, shared factory projects and ones added by hand, with who decided and when, in tabs with the cell's counts. Anyone whose key can write adds a decision by hand, pasted or as a Markdown, text or PDF file, and the person who added one or an admin deletes it. `?repo=owner/name` narrows the list to one repo. On a project, "Decisions and calls" is now "Crew questions", with the tabs "Answered by the crew" and "Answered by you".
