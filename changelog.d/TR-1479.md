In the factory edition, with an archive (the folder archive on SQLite, TARES_ARCHIVE_URL on Postgres), new `claude_code` lines are written only to the session store, not to the DuckDB events table. They still count in the source totals, and challenger lines are still kept. A factory cell on Postgres with no archive says so at start and keeps writing to DuckDB. `tares_session_lines_to_duckdb_total` counts the lines that still go there.
