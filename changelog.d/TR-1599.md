Added
Stations of a crew on Codex can now message each other (TR-1599). A station calls the Tares tool `send_to_station`; the watch reads the call from the sender's own session, so the sender is always the station that sent it, and hands the message to the recipient as a new turn between its turns. A station that is in a turn, stopped, or held at its account's limit gets its messages afterwards, once, also after a crash of the watch. A message to a station that is not in the crew comes back to its sender, and what a station sent is taken when it stops, so a station retired right after sending loses nothing. A station's message is never taken as the person's answer or consent. Claude Code crews keep SendMessage.
