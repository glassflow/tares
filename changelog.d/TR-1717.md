Reading the integrations while someone changed them could show no connection as the default, or mix settings from before and after the change (TR-1717). Each of these reads now answers from one state.
Going back to a version from before the TR-1617 move, on a cell with no state database (no TARES_STATE_DSN), shows these as they were at the first start after the move: integration connections, defaults and project choices, project secrets, guardrail rules and pairs, the merge queue, merge method, required checks, and ticket status changes waiting to be sent. What you added since looks gone, what you changed shows its old value, and what you removed is back, secrets included. On a cell first started after the move they all look empty.
The newer rows are not lost: they stay in tares.sqlite and show again when you roll forward. What you change while rolled back stays in the DuckDB file and does not show after rolling forward. A cell with a state database keeps its rows both ways.
