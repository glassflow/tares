Added
A new factory workspace now opens on a six-step first run on the Home page: decide how your crew works (who merges, how many builders, releases on or off), connect GitHub and choose where tickets live, then install Tares, sign in, start the crew and plan the first project in your terminal. Each step ticks by itself within seconds, with no reload, and a Your crew panel shows the machine, the orchestrator, the reviewer and the builders. Your choices are saved only when you confirm them (TR-1312). `tares login` and `tares factory crew up` now tell the workspace which machine they run on, with a key that may only ingest.
