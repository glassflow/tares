While the hourly usage totals were being built after an upgrade, a pass could fail with "the hourly usage totals do not match the usage rows" and start again from the beginning, or finish with a few replies counted under the wrong project. The cause: on Postgres, a reply written while a chunk of sessions was open could be missed, and session ids were compared in a different order than the database sorts them. On Postgres, a start with many replies to price could also fail with "out of shared memory", because one transaction asked for a lock per session.
