The Crew page is kept for a few seconds per person, as Home is, so several open tabs no longer
build it again each (TR-1630). The next read shows the change at once after you answer something
that waits for you (a desk question or permission, a call, a merge, a milestone, a grant, a spec
question), keep a project for later or start it again, close a project yourself, change the crew
settings, mark a station ended, accept or reject a rule proposal, add, change or remove a person,
change who may see a project, rename a project or change its icon, or delete a project. Other
changes, such as assigning a ticket or withdrawing a desk item, and what the crew itself reports,
can be up to 5 seconds later.
