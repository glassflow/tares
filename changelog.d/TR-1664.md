Fixed

The one-time move of older sessions to the archive no longer holds up incoming session lines when
another process holds a lock in Postgres: it waits half a second, then leaves the session and tries
again later. A session that changed while it moved, or that the hourly archive job held, was read and
uploaded again every second; it now waits 30 seconds, with no failed try counted. On the factory
edition, the hourly job now also removes archive part files that no session record names, once they
are 7 days old.
