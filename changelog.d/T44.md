Fixed
The merge queue brought every ready pull request that was behind its base up to date in the same pass, so all of them reran CI and, once the first merged and moved the base, all but one of those runs were thrown away. Now one pull request per repo and base is brought up to date at a time. The others keep their place in the queue and say whose turn it is ("waits its turn"), and the next one goes when that one merges, fails or leaves. A pull request that is not behind still merges at once, and projects whose CI is not GitHub's are unchanged.
