Changed
Every factory screen now says the same thing about the same state, and says whose turn it is. Ticket rows and cards, Reviews, the merge queue and `how_is_project` show who moves a ticket next: you, the crew, the checks, or another ticket it waits on. A yes reads "Next to merge" until the queue merges it. A project's count line reads like "5 merged, 2 queued, 1 stuck of 8", with a ticket closed without a merge counted apart from merged. Waiting reads "for you" everywhere, CI reads as checks, a reviewer's block reads "Reviewer stopped it", and `tares factory crew status` uses the console's words for a station. A stuck ticket is always on the desk.
