Older sessions move from DuckDB to the archive on a factory cell. After the one-time move of recent
sessions, the same background job writes every claude_code session idle for more than 7 days into
archive files (a folder, or the bucket named by TARES_ARCHIVE_URL), counts the archived lines and
marks the session verified. A Postgres cell with no TARES_ARCHIVE_URL leaves these sessions in
DuckDB and says so in the log. Nothing is deleted from DuckDB.
