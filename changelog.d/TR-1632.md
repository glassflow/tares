Each session's model replies are totalled by hour (session, hour and project) as they are written, and the crew page's per-station spend and the usage-by-project totals read those totals instead of summing every reply. A background step builds the totals for replies already stored, in chunks that resume after a restart, and the pages switch to them only once the totals match the stored replies; until then they sum the replies as before.
