Settings holds only what Tares 2.0 has (TR-1823). The Edition and Budget items are gone, and their old addresses open the nearest item left (Providers, or Usage on a self-hosted cell and General on Tares Cloud). Providers and Usage say what the model is for now: the standup summary, the milestone summaries and the weekly report; Usage names agent spend only as agent runs from before 2.0. Station telemetry (where the crew stations send their traces) is set under Monitoring on Integrations, and an old Observability or tracing address opens it there. The page's data no longer carries an edition block or a budget line.
