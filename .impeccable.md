# Tares console: design context

Read by the `impeccable` family of design skills (`/critique`, `/polish`, `/distill`, ...) and by the
`glassflow-paper-design` skill before any design work on the console (`ui/`). It sits at the repo root
because that is where those skills look. The rules an agent follows while coding are in the
"Console design" section of `AGENTS.md`; this file is the why behind them. Update it when the direction
moves, in the same PR as the code, not per PR.

> **Skills.** `glassflow-paper-design` (repo `glassflow/arche-design`, `skills/`) is the shared GlassFlow
> paper direction, used by Rius (`argus-ui`) and Tares. Rius is its source of truth; where Tares differs,
> the difference is chosen and dated below. `glassflow-etl-ui-design` is the old dark-glass system of the
> ClickHouse ETL UI; never take colour, shadow or card guidance from it here.

Each convention below is one sentence or a few, dated. "(target, TR-NNNN)" means the rule is decided but
the code does not follow it yet; that ticket brings it in. A lint gate is named once it exists.

## Design Context

### Users

Engineers who give a crew of agents real work and come back to it between other work. Tares has two
editions, and each Tares a person creates is one of them.

- **Software Factory owner, at a desk.** Plans a project with Claude Code, then checks in: what the crew
  shipped, what waits on a decision only they can make, which PR is stuck in review. Reads the Home and
  Projects pages, answers decisions, and leaves. Wants the one thing that needs them on top, and the rest
  quiet.
- **AI SRE on call.** Agents watch their services and say what broke and why. Arrives from a notification,
  often on a phone, and needs the incident, its timeline and the cause the agent found, with nothing cut
  off below 640px.
- **Keyboard or screen reader user.** State is a word, not a colour; focus is visible on every control.
- **Lead reporting upward.** Counts sessions, merged PRs and incidents for someone else, so every number
  carries its caveat (sample data, a partial window, a capped list).

Jobs, in order: see what needs me now (Home) → answer it → follow a project or incident to its end →
set up connections and rules.

### Brand Personality

Product is **Tares** (company: GlassFlow), sister to Rius and built from the same kit. Three words:
**instrument, not costume.** Warm, mechanical, opinionated: a printed standards manual with live work on
it (NASA and EPA graphics standards, the Swiss grid, Tufte). Nothing shouts, so the one thing that does
(a failure, a decision waiting on you) is unmissable. Emotional goal: calm trust in a crew you are not
watching.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Paper, not panels.** Cream ground, brown ink, content directly on the paper. Regions are separated by
  1px rules and whitespace, never by cards, shadows, glass or gradients. A panel is a 1px `border-border`.
  Floating layers (sheets, menus, popovers) cast `var(--sheet-shadow)`, and the selected item carries an
  inset accent bar; nothing else in the page has a shadow. (Chosen 2026-11-12, TR-1251. The three rgba
  card shadows in the factory edition go with TR-1266; the gate is TR-1257.)
- **Kit first.** Screens are built from `@/components/edition` (`ui/src/components/edition`). Look at
  `/dev/components` (`ui/src/pages/dev/Components.tsx`) before writing a component; a new one joins the
  kit with a gallery section. Copy from `ui/src/editions`, never from `ui/src/pages`, which predates the
  kit (TR-1250, TR-1267).
- **Type carries hierarchy.** Inter for text. Geist Mono for operational labels, table heads, state
  words, ids and code, and nothing else: buttons, body text and headings are Inter. Doto (dot-matrix) for
  headline numerals, the same face as Rius. Square geometry; `rounded-full` only for dots and switches.
  (Chosen 2026-11-12, TR-1251: align with Rius. Target: the pixel face is still Departure Mono
  (`--font-pixel`), and the legacy stylesheet still sets about 16 roles in mono.)
- **Figures, not widgets.** A chart is a numbered figure in reading order with a mono caption; a page
  opens with a running head and one sentence computed from the data. Charts default to ink.
- **States are typographic.** A mono word with a 1px coloured underline, from `StatusLabel`: ready jade,
  done plain, failed rose, live work amber, waiting and stale a dashed neutral underline in
  `--ink-soft`. (Chosen 2026-11-12, TR-1251. Target, TR-1265: today working is slate and waiting is
  amber, and three status components exist where there should be one.)
- **Colour budget.** Amber (Space Sand) means one of three things: the primary action, the selected
  item's edge, live work. Rose means failure. Colour appears only when something is wrong, selected or
  moving. Colour comes from the semantic utilities (`bg-card`, `text-muted-foreground`, `text-err`),
  never hex, `rgb()` or Tailwind palette colours (gate: TR-1256).
- **Time.** Two grammars. An instant is a UTC stamp in mono, `2026-11-12 14:05Z`, with "3h ago" in its
  tooltip. A calendar day is `Nov 12, 2026`. Never a locale string, never a relative phrase in a cell.
  (Target, TR-1261: one time module replaces the helpers spread across the editions.)
- **Words.** Edition screens use the words in `ui/src/editions/WORDS.md`, gated by `npm run test:words`.
  No em dash in any string a reader sees: an aside becomes its own sentence, an explanation takes a
  colon, a list takes commas. A lone dash glyph as the no-value mark in a cell is the one exception.
  (Chosen 2026-11-12, AGENTS.md; `src/pages` joins the check with TR-1259.)
- **Theme.** Light is the primary theme. Dark follows the kit V2 ladder, the same as Rius: canvas
  `#2b1600`, panel `#492400`, a field-focus rung `#582f06` and a segment rung `#783e06` for overlays, so
  chrome and content never share one brown and rules stay visible. (Chosen 2026-11-12, TR-1251. Target:
  the canvas is still eclipse `#1e0f00`, and one scope sets `--panel` to `--brown-850`.)
- **Motion.** Glance surfaces (Home, readouts) may animate; work surfaces (lists, detail) snap.
- **Anti-references.** Grafana-style dark cockpits with glowing accents; icon-topped card grids;
  side-stripe callouts; gradient text; sparklines as decoration; modals where a page would do.

### Design Principles

1. **Edit before you design.** The biggest wins are cuts: fewer panels, fewer columns, one grammar per
   concept. Add a component only when nothing in the kit fits.
2. **One convention per concept.** Time, table heads, state words, empty and loading states: one home
   each, written down here and in `AGENTS.md`.
3. **Colour means trouble, selection or motion.** If amber or rose is on screen, the reader can say why
   in one clause.
4. **The decision that is yours comes first.** The crew works without you; the console exists for the
   moments it cannot. Put those on top and keep the rest quiet.
5. **Numbers come with their caveat.** Sample data, partial windows, capped lists: say so beside the
   number, in the same mono voice, not in a banner.
